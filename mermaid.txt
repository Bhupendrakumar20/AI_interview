flowchart TD

%% =========================
%% USER ENTRY
%% =========================
A[User opens AI Interview Web App] --> B{Is user authenticated?}

%% =========================
%% AUTHENTICATION FLOW
%% =========================
B -- No --> C[Sign Up or Sign In Page]
C --> C1[User enters email and password]
C1 --> C2[Firebase Authentication]
C2 -- Success --> D[JWT session created]
C2 -- Failure --> C3[Authentication error shown]

B -- Yes --> D

D --> D1[Next.js middleware validates session]

%% =========================
%% DASHBOARD
%% =========================
D1 --> E[User Dashboard]
E --> E0[Fetch user interviews from Firestore]
E --> E1[View profile]
E --> E2[View interview history]
E --> E3[Start new interview]

%% =========================
%% INTERVIEW SETUP
%% =========================
E3 --> F[Interview Setup Screen]
F --> F1[Select role, domain, experience]
F1 --> F2[Load predefined question bank]
F2 --> F3[Interview config saved to Firestore]

%% =========================
%% AI INTERVIEW INITIALIZATION
%% =========================
F3 --> G[Initialize AI Interview Agent]
G --> G1[Vapi voice agent initialized]
G1 --> G2[System prompt injected]
G2 --> G3[Gemini model bound to agent]

%% =========================
%% INTERVIEW EXECUTION
%% =========================
G3 --> H[AI asks interview question]
H --> H1[User answers using voice]
H1 --> H2[Vapi speech to text conversion]
H2 --> H3[Transcript chunk stored temporarily]
H3 --> H4[Answer sent to AI processing]

%% =========================
%% AI ANALYSIS PHASES
%% =========================
H4 --> I1[Phase 1: Keyword matching]
I1 --> I2[Phase 2: Embedding similarity]
I2 --> I3[Phase 3: Contextual reasoning]
I3 --> I4[Phase 4: Score normalization]

%% =========================
%% ITERATIVE QUESTION LOOP
%% =========================
I4 --> J{More questions remaining?}
J -- Yes --> H
J -- No --> K[Interview session completed]

%% =========================
%% SCORING SYSTEM
%% =========================
K --> L[Generate final interview scores]
L --> L1[Technical score]
L --> L2[Communication score]
L --> L3[Confidence score]
L3 --> L4[Weighted final score]
L4 --> L5[Normalize score 0–10]

%% =========================
%% FEEDBACK GENERATION
%% =========================
L5 --> M[Generate structured feedback]
M --> M0[Create feedback JSON]
M0 --> M1[Strengths list]
M0 --> M2[Weaknesses list]
M0 --> M3[Improvement suggestions]

%% =========================
%% DATA STORAGE
%% =========================
M3 --> N[Persist interview data]
N --> N1[Firestore interviews collection]
N1 --> N2[User ID linked document]
N1 --> N3[Store transcript, scores, feedback]

%% =========================
%% FEEDBACK DISPLAY
%% =========================
N3 --> O[Interview feedback page]
O --> O1[Display transcript]
O --> O2[Display scores]
O --> O3[Display AI suggestions]

%% =========================
%% CONTINUOUS LEARNING LOOP
%% =========================
O --> P[Show progress comparison]
P --> E3
